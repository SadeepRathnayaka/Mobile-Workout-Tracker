# Workout Tracker

A home-screen workout app for iPhone. It shows your training split with target reps, lets you log the weight and reps for every set, and remembers what you lifted last time, so you always know the numbers to beat.

- Works offline once installed.
- All data stays on your phone. There's no account and no server.

## Install on iPhone

1. Open the app's web address in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Always launch it from the home-screen icon. It opens full-screen and works without a connection.

Data saved in the home-screen app is kept separately from a regular Safari tab, so always log your workouts from the icon.

## Home screen

- The **Up next** card at the top shows your next workout, along with how many workouts you've done this week, your total, and how long ago your last workout was.
- Below it is your split. The day after your most recent workout is marked **Next**, and a day you started but didn't finish is marked **In progress**.
- Tap a day to start it.

## Logging a workout

Each exercise card shows:

- the **target** reps for each set, for example `12 / 10 / 10 / 8`
- **Last**: the weight and reps you did in your previous session

The set rows are pre-filled with last session's weights and today's target reps.

1. Do the set, then adjust the weight or reps if they changed.
2. Tap the **check mark** to mark the set done. An exercise's card gets a highlighted edge once all its sets are done.
3. Use **+ Add set** or **- Remove set** to change how many sets you do.
4. When you're finished, tap **Finish** at the bottom. It shows how many sets you've completed, and the bar above it fills up as you go.

Only sets you ticked are saved. Everything you enter is saved as you type, so closing the app mid-workout loses nothing. To start over, use **Discard this workout** at the bottom of the day.

## Tracking progress

Tap **History** on any exercise to see:

- your **best set** (heaviest weight)
- your **last top weight**
- an **estimated 1 rep max**
- the number of sessions you've logged
- a chart of your top weight in each session
- every past session with all its sets

## Settings

Tap the gear icon to:

- switch between **kg** and **lb**. Weights you already saved are converted for display.
- **Export backup**: opens the share sheet, so you can save a backup file to Files or iCloud Drive.
- **Import backup**: restores from a backup file. It merges with what's already saved and skips duplicates.
- **Delete all workout data**.

## Backups

Your history is lost if you delete the app from your home screen or reset your phone. Export a backup every few weeks to be safe.
